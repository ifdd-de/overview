# 0001 — Expand IFDD as "Integration of Federated Data Domains"

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-25

## Context

The abbreviation `IFDD` and the domain `ifdd.de` already existed, with an earlier
meaning that no longer applies. A new expansion had to carry the architecture rather
than describe a product, work in German and English with the same letters, and stay
usable as an umbrella for work that is not purely about data.

Candidates considered: *Integration of Federated Data Domains*, *Integration ·
Federation · Decentralization · Domain orientation* as a principle formula, and
*Integration, Federation and Decentralized Data sovereignty*.

## Decision

The official expansion is **Integration of Federated Data Domains**.

`I` is fixed as *Integration*. The remaining letters name the mechanism (*federated*),
the subject (*data*) and the unit of ownership (*domains*).

## Consequences

- The name commits to data as the subject. Organisational topics are covered as
  applications of the principles, not as part of the name.
- German material uses *Integration föderierter Daten-Domänen*, the same letters.
- The abbreviation is in use elsewhere: prominently by the Institut de la Francophonie
  pour le développement durable, and as `iFDD` for the reinforcement-learning algorithm
  *incremental Feature Dependency Discovery*. Neither blocks this use, but the expansion
  always travels with the abbreviation, and the lowercase form `iFDD` is avoided.
- `[VERIFY]` Trademark registers have not been searched.
